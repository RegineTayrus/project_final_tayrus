Photos-to-PDF converter

Turn several photos of notes into one PDF for submission. It also detects text, Word, PowerPoint, Excel, and PDF files and converts them to PDF, Word, PowerPoint, and more. Everything runs on your device, so your files are never uploaded.

    Other files

      Words found in the photos

      • Choose any file. The website detects if it is a photo, text, Word, PowerPoint, Excel, or PDF file.
      • Photos: type a caption, change the order, turn them, then choose how many photos per page and click Make PDF.
      • Photos with words: click Read words from photos. The website reads the words, you can fix them, then convert them to PDF, Word, PowerPoint, or Text. This needs an internet connection.
      • Other files: choose what to convert to (for example Word to PDF, PowerPoint to PDF, text to Word) and click Convert.
      • Old .doc, .ppt and .xls files are not supported. Save them as .docx, .pptx or .xlsx first.
